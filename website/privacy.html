PRIVACY & DATA

Your information should have a clear purpose.

This notice explains the categories of information CenAI may process when you browse the website, create an account, use AI features, join the beta program or purchase a membership.

Last updated: 17 November 2026Applies to CenAI website and related services

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, CenAI may process an email address, authentication information, display name, beta application answers, feedback, support messages, project information, prompts, files you choose to submit and information needed to provide an account or membership.

2. Account and authentication data

CenAI uses Supabase services for account authentication and related application data. The website may store session tokens in browser storage so that you can remain signed in. You can sign out to end the local session. Do not share your password, one-time codes or recovery links.

3. AI prompts, files and provider processing

When you use an AI feature, the text, files or other inputs you submit may be sent to the selected AI provider or to infrastructure that routes the request. The provider and processing path depend on the feature and model you choose. Different providers have different privacy, retention and training practices. Review the relevant provider's terms and privacy documentation before sending confidential or sensitive information.

CenAI does not promise that every model runs locally or that every request stays on your device. A feature described as local or offline should be used only when the app clearly indicates that the selected model and operation are supported in that mode.

4. Payments and membership

When you purchase a membership, the payment provider processes payment credentials. CenAI receives information needed to manage the order or subscription, such as provider references, plan, payment status and membership dates. CenAI does not intend to store your full card number or PayPal password on its website.

5. Technical and operational information

Cloudflare provides website hosting, network delivery and security services. Supabase provides authentication and database services. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, request headers, device or browser details, timestamps and service logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the services. CenAI may also process service usage, quota and diagnostic events needed to operate features and prevent abuse.

6. Why information is used

  • To create and secure accounts, authenticate sessions and recover access.
  • To deliver requested AI, project, beta, account and membership features.
  • To verify payment events and maintain accurate membership status.
  • To troubleshoot errors, protect the service, enforce limits and prevent abuse.
  • To review beta applications and respond to feedback or support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

7. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, authentication, databases, AI inference, payment processing, email delivery and security. CenAI does not sell personal information as a business model. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service or others.

8. Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense advertising is not currently enabled on this website. If advertising or additional analytics are introduced, this notice will be updated to describe the relevant technologies and choices before those features are activated. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies when you interact with their services.

9. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account and payment records, resolve disputes, protect security or meet legal obligations. Retention periods can differ by data type and provider. CenAI uses technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices

You can update some account details through the account center, stop using a feature, revoke provider access where supported, or sign out. For a privacy question or a request to correct or delete information, contact CenAI through the private in-app Support area. We may need to verify the request and retain certain records where required for security, payment reconciliation or legal compliance.

11. Children and age-related use

CenAI is a general-purpose software project and is not designed as a service for children. If you are not legally able to agree to the terms that apply where you live, use the service only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to this notice

This notice may change as features, providers and legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For private privacy requests, use the Support area in the CenAI app. For general product feedback, see the Contact page. Do not place personal account information in public issue reports.
